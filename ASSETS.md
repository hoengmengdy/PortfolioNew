No assets or logos were downloaded from the reference website.

- Unsplash photography is used under the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license). It illustrates the concept and is not evidence of real product availability or affiliation. Images are locally optimized as WebP.
- The supplemental unbranded still life `collection-original.webp` was AI-generated for this project. No existing brands, labels, or product designs were used as references.
- The home hero `soap-hero.webp` and its mobile variant are original AI-generated photography of generic pink soap packaging. They follow the supplied screenshot's color direction without reproducing its branding or product labels.
- Cormorant Garamond, Jost, Noto Serif Khmer and Kantumruy Pro are from the official Google Fonts repository, under SIL Open Font License 1.1. They are subset to the weights and characters the site uses, and their license files are in `static/fonts/`.

`prepare_assets.py` documents original download/optimization steps. To run that optional development helper, install `requirements-dev.txt`. Site content explicitly identifies the collection and seeded retailers as illustrative.
